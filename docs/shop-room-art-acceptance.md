# Chronicles — Merchant room artwork acceptance

Brunna is the pictorial reference; Odo and the Guild remain vector art until genuinely clean painterly replacements are ready.

- Source: one standalone, panoramic room **without** baked-in title, menus, tabs, currency, product listings, tooltips or watermark. Those belong to the real React interface.
- Target 16:9-ish source, at least 1500×800 pixels (ideally Brunna's 1672×941). Local WebP less than 700 KB, with the high-quality master retained separately.
- Odo: warm, tool-rich armory and blacksmith, merchant face and torso in the central lane between commerce panes. Guild: cool, richly lit library and an archivist between the spell list and membership pane.
- Do not enlarge a tiny pixel sprite as the primary background. No remote assets, scripts, embedded HTML or raster content inside SVG.
- For each PR, check the paired Chromium screenshots `smoke-shop-ROOM-WIDTH.png` (real UI) and `smoke-shop-art-only-ROOM-WIDTH.png` (art alone) at widths 390 and 1280, plus 844 landscape. Confirm no important character feature under the panes.
- Update the source map in `chroniclesShopRooms.js`, the art acceptance assertions and the exact source expectation in `smoke/shop-room.mjs` for replacements. Do not weaken the minimum dimensions.
- The art-only screenshots are generated by ephemeral styles **inside Chromium smoke**, and cannot remove production controls.
- Verify frontend tests, real browser smoke and all three CI gates for the SHA before merging; confirm staging deployment independently through shadowops-infra.
